Roping Ari, the Game

First playable. She rides the two trolleybus wires at 79 km/h on her poles; a trolleybus ahead is on the same wires. Get over it and back onto the wires in front of it. Click the picture first so it gets the keys; press A to watch it done once, hoop and all.

The sound of her tips a traction-motor hum whose pitch follows her speed, and a chopper singing one note; the jaws click when they close. Pick a variant, then click the picture.
